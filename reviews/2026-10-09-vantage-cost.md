## Jumps, with disproofs

1. **J1 (Identification carries the comparison).** P9 compares v's and w's connections of one cut as "the same" connections to the same relata, which is u's identification (D17), graded; the lemma's content is this hypothesis. Fails if: a w above v on r and a u on both at which some connection of c at v is identified with no connection of c at w (the hypothesis out of reach; the comparison then graded).
2. **J2 (One span at w, over the two-element field).** D19 reads D4's "the distinctions v resolves form a span" as one span across all of w's relata and takes the field from D5's example and the seed. Fails if: a model of A1 to A5 and D1 to D5 in which the distinctions of two relata of one vantage admit no common span, or in which the dependency "in neither span alone" changes with the field.
3. **J3 (Zero gradient breaks the system).** Izzy's claim in X11, from the equality case to "no local system", crosses three gaps the round named (connections to resolutions; a vantage's saturation to s; C4 to s). Fails if: at a u on both, every shared cut of s costs the same at v and at w and w still bears a cut separating s from the null relation (D7) that u identifies with none of v's.
4. **J4 (Horizons are paths).** X10 and X12(ii) place the series of horizons under D9. Fails if: D9, once written, yields horizons that are not compositions (then both are restated without O2).
5. **J5 (Coherence is the mirror of the excess).** X11 defines the coherence of s to u as v's cuts of s that u identifies with no cut of w. Fails if: a system that persists to w (a cut of w separating s from the null relation, D7) while that set is empty, or dissolves to w while it is full.
