## Round one: six objections to P8, verified, answered

P8 as attacked: a memory m of N drawings of r, arranged along one relation (N places of one +1) or along N relations at one place; rank_m(r) and the density D15 forms from it are the same for the two arrangements whenever the drawings bear the same cuts, since the arrangement adds no cut and removes none; proof by the union of the drawings' resolutions, the folds among them, the dimension of the span, the modular rule for correlated drawings.

**1. The union assumes the memory keeps everything; the framework says it keeps a graded fraction.** Cited: B4 (removed) "the fraction is the memory's density on v (D15)"; D17 "a cut reaches another node only through the +1, at a density"; P4 graded "a vantage sees its own fold to the degree it remembers"; D3 says nothing about m bearing all its drawings' cuts. The arrangement is precisely a difference in what is remembered, and the proof wrote the union instead of showing the kept fraction is arrangement-independent. **Holds.** Verified against lines 32, 42, 68. The proof assumed density one on every drawing. Answer: the statement is split. What follows from the definitions is that rank depends on the kept families and not on their indexing (C6, below); whether the kept fraction is the same under the two arrangements is the content of Izzy's claim and is not derivable from D13 as a definition, so it is stated as the conjecture X9 with its disproof: a memory that keeps a different fraction of the same drawing at the same grade along two orderings. The third disproof route the objection names is now X9's.

**2. The second arrangement is either not a D13 memory or the first renamed.** D13 has one ordering relation, the +1, "any dimension that orders serves". Reading (a): time is the +1 and N drawings sit at one place of it, so nothing orders them and there are N ordering relations where D13 has one; reading (b): the N relations are the ordering, so both arrangements are "drawings ordered along the +1" and P8 restates D13's clause. Under (b) P8 does not reach the claim, which has N dimensions and one fixed time, N + 1 relations. **Holds.** Verified against line 29. Answer: under (b) the result is a corollary of D13 read through D5, and it is now written as one (C6), not as a proposition; that is a downgrade in kind; round two, objection 3, corrected the rest of this answer: D13's clause licenses naming the +1 and carries no rank statement, so nothing of the claim was "already in the framework". The N + 1 case is a composition of orderings and is pending O2, stated so in X9.

**3. The ordering relation is m (D13 "the ordering relation is the +1", D1 "the vantage is the fictitious dimension +1"), so "contributes no cut of its own" contradicts step one, where m makes the folds; "place" is undefined; the B1 gloss is not in B1; "B1(a) returning" is a misattribution.** **Holds.** Verified against lines 17, 18, 29, 70. Answer: the two kinds of cut m makes are separated in C6: the folds among the drawings' cuts of r, which are in res_m(r), and the cuts among the drawings themselves, their ordering, which are cuts of the drawings' identities (D2 applied to the ordering relation) and enter res_m(drawings), not res_m(r). "Place" is defined as that: a cut of the ordering telling one drawing from the others. The B1 gloss is withdrawn; the disproof names the ordering's cut entering res_m(r), which is D2 applied, not B1(a).

**4. "The same cuts" across two arrangements is a sameness at a vantage u on both (D17, graded; P7), and inside m whether two drawings' cuts are one element or two of the union is m's fold, the quantity counted, not a set-theoretic given; D12, D17, P7 missing from the premises.** **Holds.** Verified against lines 32, 46. Answer: C6 is stated for one memory's drawings rearranged, at m, where the identification is m's fold and is part of what the span is taken over; the comparison of two memories is P7's and is not what C6 claims. D12, D17, P7 added to the premises.

**5. The folds are counted one level too low, and the span formula is the memoryless picture applied to a memory (B1 removed: "a vantage with memory is the non-linear one"); D5 is pending O3.** Verified against lines 28, 29, 70, 22. **Holds** (first written "holds in part", corrected by round two, objection 5: the fold's shared term is an element of the vantage on m by D12, so "taken at m" was one level low, and C6's folds were between cuts m holds). On the picture: holds; C6 is labelled LINEAR PICTURE in its title and says in its scope that D4's relational wording at a memory is O2 and rank_m's existence is O3. It is what D5 gives in the limit where D5 applies.

**6. The modular rule as written in D5 is false for sets (over the two-element field {a, b} and {a + b} share no element and one dimension); "correlation scale" and "index set" are undefined; a count must be a ratio of ranks; the last sentence extends past N drawings.** Verified: the counter-example is correct, and D5's wording was wrong for sets since 2026-10-06. **Holds.** Answer: D5 amended to spans with the counter-example in the line; "correlation scale" dropped (C6 says only that dependent cuts count by the rank of their span along either indexing, which is D5 itself); the different-counts sentence dropped.
