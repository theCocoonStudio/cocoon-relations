## 2. Axioms (declared)

1. **The primitive is a relation.** A dimension is an information system and also a relation between two dimensions. Vertex and edge are the same kind of thing. Fully recursive.
2. **Existence is relational.** A thing exists to another only through a relation. "Everything" is the closed system with no external relation; no "outside everything."
3. **No bottom, no top: the principle of exhaustion.** If a rank is available, it exists. "Available" means available from the math; the math matches the paper but does not require it. Available and _is_ are synonymous, so "available" is not a concept. Consistency is the only existence condition. Denying a rank is a decision for a bottom, so exhaustion is the refusal of a decision, not a decision. Possible/actual is not a distinction in the structure; "possible but not actual" is a vantage statement.
4. **Arity ≥ 2.** Otherwise unchosen. Do not sneak binary assumptions in.
5. **Nothing is discrete on its own.** Everything underneath is continuous and statistical. Discreteness enters only through closure (loops, §4).

## 3. Definitions and consequences

- **Three vantages on any relation.** Inside: mechanism, closed off. Outside: the trace, i.e. the relation regarded as a dimension by a third dimension. On: the relation's own distinctions, the boundary. Confirmable means the theory makes claims about the outside view that the outside view can check.
- **Distinction** is the content of information (_mine_, accepted as semantics). What is borne on a relation is a distribution over distinctions, not a distinction.
- **Proof by preservation, not construction.** No bottom means no base case. Results must be invariants: stated at every level and shown to be kept by composition. Composition therefore carries the whole method.
- **Null relation.** Exists (required for closure, §4). It is the zero of the structure. Including it makes "exists relative to" graded, the germ of a quantity. Izzy's division-by-zero leap lives here: division by zero is what happens at the null relation.
- **Units are vantage-relative.** Whatever a vantage cannot see into counts as one from there. Zero is the null relation; the unit is the coarsest closed view. Units are relative in the same sense existence is.
- **Informational point.** Any relation, from a vantage that resolves exactly one distinction of it. Same object as the bool, the coarsest closed view, and the outside view of any open relation. A vantage outcome, not a kind of thing. A geometric point claims to be that from every vantage, hence a bottom, hence does not exist. Izzy: this is a consequence of the axioms, not a formalization. What exists has arity 2; what is measured has arity 1; measurements are not real but allowed in the meta space (thought).
- **Rank.** The number of independent distinctions a vantage resolves in a relation. Independent: neither conditioned on the other from that vantage. Rank 1 is the informational point. Rank does not exist in reality (continuous); it exists in the meta vantage, which always exists by exhaustion. No rank 0.
- **Scale and resolution** are not parameters of a measurement, they are the measurement. Resolution is where the observing relation stops seeing inside. Different resolution = different relation. Scale as a number is a comparison of two vantages, so scale is a relation between relations. Nothing has a scale-free description. The drawing on paper is a vantage; its dots are relations whose resolution the paper exhausted.
- **Open things are informational points.** A line or plane, meaning anything unclosed, has no inside/outside and presents one distinction from any vantage. Informationally a line is a point, a plane is a point. Open relations exist to what they intersect and have order only from "on."
- **Intersections are defined by what intersects,** not by their geometry. A crossing drawn as a dot is the outside view. A line-as-intersection and a line-as-dimension are different objects.
- **Approximation** is a vantage move. There is no true value from nowhere; the exact limit is the answer from a vantage no relation can occupy; the residual between the exact and the finite is the relation between two vantages, a quantity, not noise. A formal system is a vantage; "exact" means exact from there. Checkable claim (_mine_): the residuals that matter sit across vantage changes, the same place as infinities and division by zero.
- **Core difference from existing frameworks.** Each fixed one vantage implicitly (absolute time, preferred scale, external apparatus), so a residual across vantages could only be seen by leaving the framework. Here the vantage is a term; a change of vantage is a relation between relations inside the theory; the residual is computed, not discovered by the theory dying. Success = vantage changes that used to cost a new theory become relations inside this one, with the residual predicted before the clocks are good enough.
- **The framework tells us nothing, by design.** Content lives in vantages. A single vantage's resolution map is chosen freely (arity 1, a measurement). Izzy: the choices affect a relation between vantages that has bound rules; content is in the relation between maps (arity 2). _Parked as a prediction_ (§7) because "choice," "effect" and "cause" carry time.

## Leads
