## 2. Axioms (declared)

1. **The primitive is a relation.** A dimension is an information system and also a relation between two dimensions. Vertex and edge are the same kind of thing. Fully recursive.
2. **Existence is relational.** A thing exists to another only through a relation. "Everything" is the closed system with no external relation; no "outside everything."
3. **No bottom, no top: the principle of exhaustion.** If a rank is available, it exists. "Available" means available from the math; the math matches the paper but does not require it. Available and _is_ are synonymous, so "available" is not a concept. Consistency is the only existence condition. Denying a rank is a decision for a bottom, so exhaustion is the refusal of a decision, not a decision. Possible/actual is not a distinction in the structure; "possible but not actual" is a vantage statement.
4. **Arity ≥ 2.** Otherwise unchosen. Do not sneak binary assumptions in.
5. **Nothing is discrete on its own.** Everything underneath is continuous and statistical. Discreteness enters only through closure (loops, §4).

## 3. Definitions and consequences

- **Three vantages on any relation.** Inside: mechanism, closed off. Outside: the trace, i.e. the relation regarded as a dimension by a third dimension. On: the relation's own distinctions, the boundary. Confirmable means the theory makes claims about the outside view that the outside view can check.
- **Distinction** is the content of information (_mine_, accepted as semantics). What is borne on a relation is a distribution over distinctions, not a distinction.
- **Proof by preservation, not construction.** No bottom means no base case. Results must be invariants: stated at every level and shown to be kept by composition. Composition therefore carries the whole method.
- **Null relation.** Exists (required for closure, §4). It is the zero of the structure. Including it makes "exists relative to" graded, the germ of a quantity. Izzy's division-by-zero leap lives here: division by zero is what happens at the null relation.
- **Units are vantage-relative.** Whatever a vantage cannot see into counts as one from there. Zero is the null relation; the unit is the coarsest closed view. Units are relative in the same sense existence is.
- **Informational point.** Any relation, from a vantage that resolves exactly one distinction of it. Same object as the bool, the coarsest closed view, and the outside view of any open relation. A vantage outcome, not a kind of thing. A geometric point claims to be that from every vantage, hence a bottom, hence does not exist. Izzy: this is a consequence of the axioms, not a formalization. What exists has arity 2; what is measured has arity 1; measurements are not real but allowed in the meta space (thought).
- **Rank.** The number of independent distinctions a vantage resolves in a relation. Independent: neither conditioned on the other from that vantage. Rank 1 is the informational point. Rank does not exist in reality (continuous); it exists in the meta vantage, which always exists by exhaustion. No rank 0.
- **Scale and resolution** are not parameters of a measurement, they are the measurement. Resolution is where the observing relation stops seeing inside. Different resolution = different relation. Scale as a number is a comparison of two vantages, so scale is a relation between relations. Nothing has a scale-free description. The drawing on paper is a vantage; its dots are relations whose resolution the paper exhausted.
- **Open things are informational points.** A line or plane, meaning anything unclosed, has no inside/outside and presents one distinction from any vantage. Informationally a line is a point, a plane is a point. Open relations exist to what they intersect and have order only from "on."
- **Intersections are defined by what intersects,** not by their geometry. A crossing drawn as a dot is the outside view. A line-as-intersection and a line-as-dimension are different objects.
- **Approximation** is a vantage move. There is no true value from nowhere; the exact limit is the answer from a vantage no relation can occupy; the residual between the exact and the finite is the relation between two vantages, a quantity, not noise. A formal system is a vantage; "exact" means exact from there. Checkable claim (_mine_): the residuals that matter sit across vantage changes, the same place as infinities and division by zero.
- **Core difference from existing frameworks.** Each fixed one vantage implicitly (absolute time, preferred scale, external apparatus), so a residual across vantages could only be seen by leaving the framework. Here the vantage is a term; a change of vantage is a relation between relations inside the theory; the residual is computed, not discovered by the theory dying. Success = vantage changes that used to cost a new theory become relations inside this one, with the residual predicted before the clocks are good enough.
- **The framework tells us nothing, by design.** Content lives in vantages. A single vantage's resolution map is chosen freely (arity 1, a measurement). Izzy: the choices affect a relation between vantages that has bound rules; content is in the relation between maps (arity 2). _Parked as a prediction_ (§7) because "choice," "effect" and "cause" carry time.

## 4. Provisional constructions (from the drawing; to be made informational)

- **Loops.** A closed relation presents as one thing from outside because outside cannot see the going-around; from "on" it is continuous. Candidate definition (_mine_): a relation whose composition with itself, at some order, presents as null from outside. Counting lives only at relations. Quantization would mean: closed relations count from outside, open ones do not.
- **Phase.** A loop has a phase: direction along it, and the handedness of the loops around it (right-hand rule). Izzy: this is how they have always thought of it. The natural landing place for the complex-numbers leap.
- **No smallest loop.** A loop with no loops around it has no phase, so is not a loop. (Izzy caught my error.) Izzy: "a point with spin," phase kept and inside denied, is the problem particle physics has today; the self-interaction infinities are the division-by-zero signal, subtracted instead of read.
- **The 3+1 argument.** Izzy: points cannot exist; the minimal information-bearing thing is closed (inside, boundary, outside); a series of points cannot exist; minimal rank is 3; +1 is the rank needed to perceive the intersection. _Mine_, strongest reconstruction: 3 follows from what a phase needs. Direction along needs rank 1; handedness around needs two more; a circle in a plane has a sign, not a phase. Inside/outside is vantage-rank-relative (a closed curve separates rank 2, a closed surface rank 3, nothing in rank 4), so "has an inside" is a property of thing plus vantage; the perceiving relation must be one rank up: the +1. Izzy agrees the phase requirement carries the 3 and agrees on the +1 direction. Under exhaustion, 3 is a floor, not a count: "3+1 observed" is a resolution question (why the lab vantage resolves three), not an existence one.
- **Intersections in rank 3, firmed 2026-09-06** (Izzy asked to close the phase hole). Primitive = closed thing that separates; in ambient rank n that forces rank n−1, so "separates" is now a definition (rank one below ambient), not a borrowing. Two closed surfaces in rank 3 meet generically in a set of loops (possibly knotted or linked), never an open line, never a point; points only by tangency (measure zero, and a tiny loop looks like one from far). A sphere is not an intersection of two surfaces except by coincidence (same relation twice); it can bound two overlapping regions, but regions are the ambient. The along-direction of the loop is fixed by the two normals and flips when the pair is swapped. **Corrected on Izzy's challenge (2026-09-06):** two ordered normals pick a direction only via an ambient orientation; pair order, along-direction and ambient handedness are three things of which any two fix the third. "Around" = linking: a circle-valued phase exists when the linkers are circles (informational). Counting: k closed hypersurfaces in rank n meet generically in rank n−k. A loop with one along-direction needs n = arity + 1; with arity ≥ 2 that is 3. Rank 4: two primitives meet in a surface (two along-directions, no single phase). Rank 2: points (around, no along). **Rank 3 is the only rank where the generic relation of two primitives has one along and one circle around: a full phase.** Arity 3 in rank 3: points; three ordered normals define an orientation, but a single such relation has no handedness on its own; two of them compare (same or opposite) with no external reference. **Handedness is a distinction between two arity-3 relations, not on one.** Both hands exist by exhaustion; the right-hand rule is a naming from a vantage, not an assumption. Lead (mine): the third primitive is the +1, the perceiver, and what it reads is a signed point, i.e. a measurement. Still from the drawing: "generic" (coincidence has measure zero), which needs the continuous distribution over relations from axiom 5, not yet used.

## Leads

1. **How 3+1 was approached.** Müller & Masanes: assumed a qubit as the minimal information carrier plus continuous reversible dynamics and derived 3 spatial dimensions; ended at space, not at time or signature. Tegmark: argued 3+1 is the only signature with well-posed hyperbolic field equations, so prediction is possible; anthropic in flavour, not derived from measurement. Neither starts from a definition of measurement. What to learn: which assumptions about the carrier or the observer did the dimensional work. Izzy is starting from scratch on this (§4).
2. **Information causality.** Where it ended: Tsirelson's bound recovered, but no derivation of full QM from it, and the principle is stated in terms of bits and channels, so it assumes classical communication as a primitive. What to learn: how an informational principle sets a physical constant.
3. **Frieden's EPI.** Where it ended: known Lagrangians rewritten as Fisher information; the variational principle (tuned J, hand-set κ) not accepted as a derivation. What lived: Hall & Reginatto (Schrödinger from Fisher information plus an exact-uncertainty principle); Caticha's entropic dynamics (physics as inference, configuration space put in by hand); Chentsov's theorem (Fisher metric is the unique metric invariant under distinguishability-preserving maps) and Wootters 1981 (for quantum states it is the Fubini-Study metric). Chentsov is the measurement-first, dimension-free result; flagged to Izzy as worth their time.
