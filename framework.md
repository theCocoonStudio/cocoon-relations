# Framework

Textbook style: axioms, definitions, propositions with premise lists, conjectures, bifurcations, must-reproduce, open items. The trunk is what depends on axioms and definitions only. Draft 0 is from 2026-09-06; the verdicts of the first review (`reviews/2026-10-05-first-review.md`) are to be folded in by PR, one bifurcation at a time.

**Conventions.** A = axiom. D = definition. P = proposition, always with a premise list and a proof. C = corollary. X = conjecture, stated but not derived. B = bifurcation, a named choice point with branches. M = must-reproduce, empirical, never assumed. O = open item. Every item lists what it depends on; the **trunk** is what depends on A and D only; anything else names its branch. Attribution in brackets. Notation is borrowed from set theory and function application where convenient and is provisional. This document is written from a meta-vantage (thought), where arity-1 statements are allowed; the object level is continuous (A5), so classical two-valued logic here is a vantage on the structure, not the structure.

## Axioms

- **A1 (Relation).** There is a class R. Every element of R is a relation among elements of R. Nothing else is in R. [Izzy]
- **A2 (Relational existence).** For r, s ∈ R: r exists to s iff some t ∈ R has both r and s among its relata. There is no existence other than existence-to. [Izzy]
- **A3 (Exhaustion, relative).** For every vantage v: whatever is consistent with what v resolves exists to v. Consistency is judged by the vantage over what it bears; there is no global consistency condition and none is needed. [Izzy; relativised 2026-10-05 after the first review showed the absolute form admits the relation of all relations not among their own relata, Russell's contradiction. Izzy: index membership to the vantage and the singularity is read, not removed: the self-vantage (D10) is where the absolute form divided by zero, and the absolute quantifier that remains is O4.]
- **A4 (Arity).** Every relation has at least two relata. Arity is otherwise unconstrained. [Izzy]
- **A5 (Multiplicity).** What a relation bears is a multiplicity of distinctions, never a single one; discreteness is not primitive. Quantity (a measure over distinctions) is derived, not assumed: the first review found that "distribution" imported the real numbers, a bottom made of numbers. [Izzy; weakened 2026-10-05]

## Definitions

- **D1 (Vantage).** A vantage on r is any v ∈ R with r among its relata. Every relation has three views: inside(r), on(r), and outside_v(r) for each vantage v. A vantage is not a point in a space; it is the relation itself, the "fictitious dimension +1" to what it measures (Izzy, 2026-10-06). Its linear picture is a projection from an ambient one rank up onto the vantage's span: B1.a as a picture of D1, not a premise of it. [Izzy; naming mine] Depends: A1.
- **D2 (Distinction).** A distinction is a cut: binary by definition, it tells two things apart, and it is made by a vantage, not found in the relation. Information is the cut, and it is the only categorical thing in R (Izzy, 2026-10-06: "relations + probability. information is what's categorical"). The earlier wording, an element of the support of what r bears, put the cut in the data; withdrawn. [Izzy; the wording mine] Depends: A5, D1.
- **D16 (Data).** The data of r is r itself: what it bears, with no cut in it and no number attached, since any number is some vantage's count of cuts. Data has no rank. [Izzy, 2026-10-06; the formal reading mine] Depends: A1, A5.
- **D3 (Resolution).** res_v(r) is the set of distinctions of r that v bears. [derived] Depends: D1, D2.
- **D4 (Independence, structural).** The distinctions v resolves form a span; two of them are independent from v iff they are linearly independent in it. Relationally, with no ambient: neither is a composition of the other with what v bears. Probability plays no part; graded answers come from D14. [Izzy: "the math bears out with simple linear algebra", 2026-10-06; the relational wording mine] Depends: D3, D9 (pending O2 for the relational wording). Resolves O1.
- **D5 (Rank).** rank_v(r) is the dimension of the span of res_v(r): the number of mutually independent distinctions. For two spans A and B of distinctions: dim(A + B) = dim A + dim B − dim(A ∩ B), the modular rule; for two sets of cuts the intersection is of their spans, not of the sets (over the two-element field, {a, b} and {a + b} share no element and one dimension; amended 2026-10-09, reviews/2026-10-09-density-conversion.md, round one, objection 6); the shared term is the fold (D11). [mine; the modular rule is Izzy's linear algebra] Depends: D4. **Pending O3.**
- **D6 (Informational point).** r is a point to v iff rank_v(r) = 1. [Izzy: a consequence, not a formalization] Depends: D5.
- **D7 (Null relation).** 0 ∈ R is the relation that bears no distinction to any vantage. Exists by A3 if consistent. [Izzy: units-level; mine: the zero] Depends: A3, D3.
- **D8 (Closure).** r is closed iff some composition of r with itself is indistinguishable from 0 to some vantage. [mine] Depends: D7, **D9 pending O2.**
- **D9 (Composition).** Pending O2. [open]
- **D11 (Fold).** Two distinctions of r independent to one vantage and dependent to v have folded at v: in the linear picture, their difference lies in v's kernel, the direction a projection loses. The fold is the shortfall of rank at v. [the name from the paper analogy, 2026-10-06; Izzy's: the fold is what the +1 sees] Depends: D4, D5.
- **D12 (The level up).** What two distinctions share at v is not a distinction of v. It is a distinction of a vantage on v, the level up, where every intersection at v is an element. Independence is disjointness at a level, read for spans (two sets of cuts can be disjoint and dependent, {a, b} and {a + b}; amended 2026-10-09 with D5); the intersections are the next level's elements. [Izzy, 2026-10-06: a mutual intersection is a node; the node is the vantage on the two] Depends: D1, D11. The recursion this sets up is O3, with content.
- **D13 (Memory).** A memory is a vantage whose relata are vantages (drawings) ordered along a relation: the drawing of drawings. The ordering relation is the +1; "time" is its usual name and any dimension that orders serves (Izzy). The vantage that sees a fold and the vantage that remembers are the same object. [Izzy: memory requires a +1; the identification with the level up, mine] Depends: D1, D12.
- **D14 (Grade).** The grade of a prediction by a memory m of a distinction hidden from a drawing d is a ratio of ranks: the rank of what m resolves that agrees with d, over the rank of m. A count over a count; no number is imported. Answers in this framework are graded by default, not categorical (Izzy, 2026-10-06, standing rule). [Izzy: the answer is probabilistic; the ratio of ranks, mine] Depends: D5, D13. **Pending B5:** the grade depends on how finely m resolves sameness between its own drawings.
- **D15 (Density).** The density of a vantage v on r relative to a vantage w is dim I_v(r) / dim I_w(r), the ratio of their counts of cuts: a scale (Izzy: "resolution is a number, scale"), never a ratio over the data, which has no rank (D16). Saturation is a vantage no vantage in play out-cuts, which is a node to the level up. [Izzy's October density and saturation, and the correction of 2026-10-06; the formal reading mine] Depends: D14, D16. Replaces the information-over-data ratio of notes §10, which needed a measure, and the first version of this line, which measured against the data.
- **D17 (Sameness across vantages).** A cut of v and a cut of w are the same to u, a vantage on both (D12), iff u does not distinguish them: identity across vantages is the fold of the +1 (D11), and it is graded, the fraction of v's cuts that u identifies with cuts of w, a ratio of ranks (D14). No point is the same on two drawings, because a point is a rank-1 outcome (D6), not a thing; only a cut can be. Epistemic reading, Izzy's: "my ones and zeros don't carry the same rules in another node, only probabilistically": a cut reaches another node only through the +1, at a density. [Izzy, 2026-10-07; the definition form mine] Depends: D6, D11, D12, D14. Pending O2 for the relational wording of u.
- **D10 (Self-vantage).** A relation whose relata are the same relation, r(a, a). It is a vantage on itself (D1) and bears exactly one distinction to every vantage: it is the geometric point, the tautology, the zero of self-vantage beside the null relation (D7). Admitted, not excluded: it is what makes A3 consistent, because a question a vantage asks about itself has no content there and a plain answer from any other vantage. [Izzy's October "a self intersection only is a tautology"; the admission and its role, 2026-10-05]
- **D18 (Two vantages on one relation: the shared relata, and inclusion).** Two vantages v and w on the same r (D1) share the relata in relata(v) ∩ relata(w), which holds r at least; relata are elements of R, so this intersection is set identity at the meta-vantage (Conventions) and needs no u. w is above v on r (inclusion) iff the shared relata are all of v's relata. A cut of r is shared to u, a vantage on both (P7), iff u identifies a cut v bears with a cut w bears (D17): the sharing of cuts is graded and has no referent without u (P7). Inclusion is of relata, what is available, never of resolution, what is extracted (D3); a superset of resolutions would make w out-cut v on r, and nothing below assumes it: a cut of r that v bears and w does not is admitted, and it is where Izzy's "detail" sits (C7). A higher vantage is a second system on r, not a memory of v (D13) and not the level up on v (D12). [Izzy, 2026-10-09: "concentric circles, and the higher vantage's information space is a superset"; "the level up is not the same information system"; 20:14: "the general form isnt a subset, its an intersection. the only thing verifiable is the subset"; the definition form mine; Claude's reading of "verifiable", marked: under inclusion every relatum v connects to is among w's, so every connection v makes has a candidate at w for u to identify, and which u identifies is graded] Depends: D1, D3; D17 and P7 for shared cuts. Restated 2026-10-09 from the superset form after round two (objections 1, 7) and round three (1 to 3: the intersection of cuts needs u; "space" dropped) (`reviews/2026-10-09-vantage-cost.md`).
- **D19 (Cost of a reading).** The distinctions w resolves, of all its relata, form one span S_w (D4: "the distinctions v resolves form a span"), taken over the two-element field as in D5's example and the formal seed, provisionally. For a cut c of r that w bears (D3) and another relatum x of w, a connection of c at w to x is a distinction w resolves that lies in the span of c together with res_w(x) and in neither span alone: a cut that depends on c and on w's cuts of x jointly. cost_w(c) is the dimension (D5) of the span of the set of all connections of c at w, over all of w's other relata: one count, indexed by the vantage that pays it. Two costs compare only at a vantage u on both (P7): u reads cost_w(c) as the rank at u of all of w's connections of c and cost_v(c) likewise, a connection u identifies across the two (D17) counted once in each; compared by difference (D5) or as a ratio (D14). A connection is one cut at w and the unit is P2's, vantage-relative; no clock and no speed enter. Replaces the missing term of notes §15.3. [Izzy, 2026-10-09: "the higher vantage has to traverse more relations, on average, to read the point", then "making connections with it to every node the lower sphere has AND its own"; the rank form mine] Depends: D2, D3, D4, D5; the comparison clause P7, D17, D14. Pending O3 (D5) and, for the comparison, O2 (D12's relational wording, via P7 and D17) and B5 (D14). Restated after round two (objections 4, 12) and round three (4 to 7: the pair and the P6 citation dropped, the one span from D4, the field marked, the comparison made one rule).

## Propositions

- **P1 (No bottom).** No r ∈ R has a relatum outside R. Premises: A1. Proof: A1 states it. Trunk. [derived]
- **C1 (A bottom is unverifiable).** "r has no inside" is not a statement any vantage can verify. Premises: A1, D1, D3, D6. Proof: if r had no inside, res_v(r) would be a single distinction for every v. But res_v(r) is also a single distinction for any r whose further distinctions v does not bear. The two cases are identical in every outside view. Trunk. [derived; mine]
- **C2 (No top).** For every v ∈ R there is a w ∈ R with v among its relata. Premises: A1, A3. Proof: such a w is consistent with A1–A5, hence in R by A3. Trunk. [derived]
- **C3 (Nothing is everything).** The total relation (everything: no external relation, A2) and the null relation (D7: no distinction to any vantage) are indistinguishable from outside, because there is no outside of either. Premises: A2, D7. Proof: an outside view is a vantage with the relation among its relata; the total relation has none by A2; the null relation presents none by D7; the two outside views are both empty. Trunk. [Izzy's "nothing is everything", 2026-10-03; the proposition form, mine]
- **P3 (The paradox is a self-vantage).** The relation r* whose relata, to v, are the relations not among their own relata to v, is among its own relata to w iff not, only when w = r*, i.e. only from the self-vantage, where by D10 no distinction is borne. From every other vantage the question has one answer. Premises: A3 (relative), D1, D10. Proof sketch: the defining condition quantifies over facts to v; the question is a fact to w; the contradiction requires w to be the vantage in its own condition, which is D10. Trunk, **sketch**: a model must show that no relation other than the self-relation can be its own vantage at the same level (J0). [Izzy's move; the write-up, mine]
- **P4 (A linear vantage cannot see its own fold).** No distinction of v tells v that two of its distinctions have folded. Premises: D4 (linear), D11. Proof: the fold is v's kernel; a projection carries no trace of its kernel in its image. Corollary: the fold is visible only to a vantage on v (D12), or, graded, to a memory of v (D13, D14). Trunk. [the theorem is linear algebra; its place here, mine; Izzy: the answer is graded, not categorical] **Sketch** for the relational wording, pending O2. **Graded form (2026-10-06, by the fork principle on B1):** a vantage sees its own fold to the degree it remembers; the memoryless vantage is the linear limit.
- **C4 (Nothing is everything, graded).** A vantage of density zero (the self-relation, D10) and a vantage of density one (saturation, D15) resolve nothing new to the level up; from outside they are indistinguishable, which is C3 said as the two ends of one graded quantity. Something is an interior grade. Premises: C3, D10, D15. Proof: at density zero nothing is resolved; at density one everything is and nothing remains to resolve; the level up sees neither change. Not trunk until D13–D15 are. [Izzy's "nothing is everything" and the fork principle; this reading, mine]
- **C5 (The fork principle), SKETCH.** A fork of the framework is removed by a grade. Premises: P5 (the math has a rank), D12 (a fold belongs to the level up), D14 (the level up answers in grades). Proof sketch: a fork is two options the framework cannot separate from its axioms, i.e. a fold of the framework as a vantage; by D12 it is a distinction of the level up; by D14 that distinction is graded. Cost of failure: the negation is a fork binary from every vantage, an absolute distinction with content; the only absolute distinction in R is the empty one (D10), so a categorical fork with content is a bottom. The principle is no-bottom said for distinctions. Not trunk until D13–D15 are; sketch until O2 gives D12 its relational wording. [Izzy's principle; the derivation and the cost, mine, 2026-10-06]
- **P6 (No cut alone).** No vantage cuts a relation on its own; the smallest thing that cuts is two held together, a memory of two (D13) or a mutual intersection. Information exists from the pair up and not at the bottom. Premises: A4, D2, D10, D12. Proof: a relation to itself (D10) tells nothing apart; a cut tells two things apart, so it needs two relata (A4) and belongs to the relation that has both (D12). Trunk once D12 has its relational wording. [Izzy, 2026-10-06, predicted by Claude from Izzy's October "a mutual intersection is a node" and confirmed; the proposition form, mine]
- **P7 (The seam is composition).** With no ambient (B1 removed), the only comparison of two vantages v and w is a vantage u on both; any "sum over" or "same point in" the pictures of v and w presupposes u, and without u it has no referent. Premises: D1, D12, B1 (removed), D17. Proof: a comparison is a relation with v and w among its relata (D1); such a relation is the level up (D12); "the same point" needs an ambient to be a thing, which B1's removal denies, so it reduces to D17's sameness of cuts, which is u's fold. Trunk once D12 has its relational wording. The micro–macro seam (notes §14) is this proposition read from the lab: quantum theory draws a spread over drawings on a fixed paper, gravity draws the paper, and the Math fails at summing drawings that share no paper, which is O2. [mine, 2026-10-07, from Izzy's "guide me on exactly where the Math fails between the micro and macro"]
- **P5 (The math has a rank).** The formalism that states this framework is in R, hence a vantage, hence of some rank to every other vantage; at its own self-vantage (D10) it resolves nothing. Premises: A1, C2, D1, D5, D10. Proof: C2 forbids a top, so the formalism is a relatum of some relation and a relation itself; D5 applies. Trunk. [Izzy's prediction, 2026-10-06: "our math itself must be a relation, as an object"; the proposition form, mine]
- **P2 (Units are vantage-relative).** The distinction counted as one by v is whatever r has rank_v(r) = 1; a different vantage may give the same r a different rank. Premises: D5, D6. Proof: rank is indexed by v by construction. Trunk. Pending O1. [derived]
- **P9 (Cost grows with inclusion, on the shared cuts; a lemma).** Let w be above v on r (D18), u a vantage on both (P7), and c a cut of r shared to u (D18). Hypothesis: u identifies each connection of c at v to a relatum x with a connection of c at w to the same x (D17). Then cost_w(c) ≥ cost_v(c), read as ranks at u (D19), and the difference is the rank at u of w's connections of c that u identifies with none of v's, modulo the span of the identified ones (D5's modular rule): connections to relata v lacks, or to shared relata where w bears cuts v does not; the difference is zero iff those depend at u on the identified ones (D4). Premises: D18, D19, D17, D4, D5. Proof: under the hypothesis the image at u of v's connections of c lies in the image of w's; the dimension of a span does not fall under inclusion and rises by the independent additions (D5, D4); v makes no connection of c to a relatum it lacks (D19). The content of the proposition is its hypothesis, the identification; given it the inequality is D5's monotonicity, so P9 is a lemma and no model of R fails it (round two, objection 3; round three, 12). What is testable is the hypothesis's reach. Fails if (reach): a w above v on r and a u on both at which some connection of c at v is identified with no connection of c at w; then the comparison is graded by the identified fraction (D17) and the lemma speaks of that fraction alone. Trunk once D12 has its relational wording (P7, D17); pending O2, O3 (D5), B5 (D14 through D17). [Izzy's theorem, 2026-10-09; the proposition form mine; restated in the intersection form after round two (1 to 3: the first found blind what Izzy wrote the same minute, "the only thing verifiable is the subset") and round three (8 to 13: the hypothesis in the statement, the difference's two sources, the Fails-if about reach, the labels propagated)]
- **C7 (Detail is cheap below, direction above).** At a vantage u on both (P7), for a shared cut c under P9's hypothesis, the cost at v is at most the cost at w (P9), and the difference is w's connections of c that u identifies with none of v's: to the relata w has and v lacks, to which v makes no connection (D19), and to shared relata where w bears cuts v does not. ## Conjectures

- **X1 (No geometric point), RESOLVED 2026-10-05.** There is exactly one: the self-relation D10, which bears no information. Every other relation has distinct relata to some vantage and so rank ≥ 2 there. The September claim "a point is a bottom, hence does not exist" becomes: a point exists and is empty. [Izzy; the earlier conjecture withdrawn by the resolution]
- **X2 (Discreteness only at closure).** Any r with a finite rank to some v is closed in the sense of D8. [Izzy] Pending O2.
- **X3 (Completeness is a density).** How much of R the formalism captures is its density (D15) from a vantage one level up: below one from every vantage but its own, where it is undefined. The categorical incompleteness results are the statement that this density is below one. [mine, from P5 and D15] Not trunk until D13–D15 are.
- **X4 (The math adjusts).** The formalism is a memory being written (D13): its axioms are the memory's current state and its rank changes along the +1. What is preserved across adjustments is which compositions count as derivations (proof by preservation, notes §1). [Izzy, 2026-10-06: "if reality is relational, the math must adjust"; the preserved invariant, mine] Depends: P5, D13. The fixed point it needs is O3.
- **X6 (The measured correlation bound is a ratio of ranks).** The graded fraction of pairings a pair's vantage resolves (B3) is the rank of the pairings its memory can hold over all of them, and the measured bound of M3 is that ratio. The first number the framework would predict, if it can be derived from its own counts. Disproof: a derived ratio that does not land at the measured value, or a ratio that depends on the pair's content rather than on the memory's rank. [mine, 2026-10-06, from Izzy's principle applied to B3]
- **X7 (The pair resolves one third of the pairing range), SPECULATION, LOW.** A ratio of finite ranks is rational; the measured bound of M3 against the classical and maximal values is an irrational fraction of the range (about 0.414), so X6 as a ratio of single ranks cannot land on M3 exactly: either ranks go to a limit, which re-imports the reals (the finite-rank condition on D14), or the ratio is placed on pairings. On pairings, whose rank is a product and so squared relative to single ranks, the same fraction is exactly 1/3: (8 − 4)/(16 − 4). Conjecture: a pair's vantage resolves one third of what lies between the classical pair and the maximal pair, and the composition rule (O2) yields that third with no tuning. Numerological until derived; the only candidate number the framework has. Disproof: a composition rule that yields any other fraction, or none; or a derivation that needs the squared placement put in by hand. [mine, 2026-10-07; the rationality finding is a disproof-shaped result against X6 as first stated]
- **X9 (The density conversion: what a memory resolves is arrangement-free).** Izzy's claim, 2026-10-09: "there is no difference in the two [measurement along one dimension and sampling several at one place], only in the interpretation."
